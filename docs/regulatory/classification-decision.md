# Regulatory Classification Decision Record

**Status:** launch blocker, specialist review required

## Intended purpose under review

NephroCare accepts clinical and laboratory inputs, calculates a CKD risk
estimate, explains contributing factors, and produces connected diet guidance.
Those claims and workflows, rather than a disclaimer, determine the regulatory
analysis. Production use for diagnosis, treatment selection, patient monitoring,
or clinical decision support is prohibited until this record is signed.

## Required decision

1. Freeze the exact intended-use statement, users, patient population, input
   provenance, outputs, and every clinical claim.
2. Have an India medical-device regulatory professional map the software against
   the Medical Devices Rules 2017, current CDSCO software guidance, and published
   software and nephrology classifications.
3. Document whether it is a medical device, its proposed class and rule, licence
   path, quality-management obligations, clinical evidence, risk management,
   cybersecurity, post-market surveillance, and adverse-event duties.
4. Obtain written legal/regulatory approval before enabling clinical or patient-
   specific use. Record approver, scope, evidence, date, and renewal trigger here.

Until approval, deployment must remain an adult-only research demonstration,
must not be promoted for clinical reliance, and must not retain real patient data.
